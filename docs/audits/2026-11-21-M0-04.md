## Findings
1. **[medium, for M1-02] Bind groups are created on every pass.** `encodeBlurPass` and `encodeBlitPass` call `createBindGroup` every frame. With one pass that's fine, but M1 runs several passes × substeps per frame, and the allocations add up. Fix: build the two ping-pong bind groups (A→B, B→A) once and pick one by parity. (→ M1-02 criterion)
2. **[low] `readBufferToFloat32` fails silently on buffers without `COPY_SRC`.** It produces a GPU validation error, not a clear JS error, and `createUniformBuffer` doesn't add `COPY_SRC`. Fix: check `buffer.usage & GPUBufferUsage.COPY_SRC` and throw a descriptive error, and document the requirement. (→ Discovered)
3. **[low, for M1-02] The uniform layout is unverified on the GPU.** TS packing and WGSL structs are maintained by hand on both sides. The first real uniform struct (M1-02 flow parameters) should get a round-trip test: a tiny compute pass that copies the uniform fields into a storage buffer, then a readback compared with the packed values. (→ M1-02 criterion)
4. **[low, for M1-05] Aspect ratio stretch.** The square grid is drawn across the whole window, so the test disk shows as an ellipse. The real renderer must letterbox the square canvas, or match the grid aspect to the canvas spec. (→ M1-05 criterion)
5. **[info]** The FPS figure couldn't be checked here because the in-app pane was hidden and throttled. Glance at the overlay in your own Chrome: 512² blur + blit should sit at your display's refresh rate.
